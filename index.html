DESTINATIONS I DREAM OF

USA

The USA is one of my biggest dream destinations. I want to experience its iconic cities and landscapes, while also pursuing my master's degree there someday. For me, America represents both a dream vacation and a step toward my future.

GERMANY

Germany attracts me because of its strong technology and engineering culture. As someone interested in AI and software development, I dream of working with a leading tech company there while experiencing German culture and exploring cities like Berlin.

FRANCE

Paris is a place I want to experience for its timeless architecture, art, cafés, and unforgettable atmosphere. I want to see the Eiffel Tower, walk through its historic streets, and experience the beauty of French culture.
